AiMama Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 20, 2026

Introduction

AiMama ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our pregnancy tracking mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Personal Information: Name, due date, birth date, weight, and height
  • Health Tracking Data: Kick counts, weight measurements, appointments, contraction timing, vitamins and supplements tracking, photo journal (pregnancy bump photos)
  • Family & Friends Information: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and relationship types of people you choose to share updates with (stored locally on your device)
  • Chat Messages: Questions and conversations with our AI assistant

Device Permissions

AiMama may request the following permissions to provide its features:

  • Camera Access: To take bump photos for your pregnancy photo journal (optional feature)
  • Photo Library Access: To select photos from your device gallery for your photo journal (optional feature)
  • Media Library Access: To save your bump photos to your device gallery for backup (optional feature)
  • Notifications: To remind you to take weekly bump photos and receive appointment reminders (optional feature)
  • Calendar Access: To create and share appointment reminders with your family and friends (optional feature)
  • SMS/Messaging: To send pregnancy updates and appointment reminders to your chosen contacts via SMS or WhatsApp (optional feature)

You can choose to grant or deny these permissions at any time. The app will function without these permissions, but sharing features will not be available.

How We Store Your Data

Local Storage: All of your personal information and tracking data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not store this data on our servers.

AI Chat: When you use the AI Assistant feature, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to generate responses. These messages include your name and current pregnancy week for context. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this data: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized pregnancy tracking and information
  • To calculate your current pregnancy week and due date
  • To generate relevant AI assistant responses based on your pregnancy stage
  • To display your health tracking data and statistics
  • To send appointment reminders and pregnancy updates to contacts you choose (only when you explicitly use the sharing features)
  • To create calendar events that you can share with your family and friends (only when you use the calendar integration feature)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Only when you use the AI Assistant feature, to generate responses to your questions
  • Your Device's Messaging Apps: Only when you explicitly choose to send messages to your family and friends through SMS or WhatsApp. These messages are sent directly from your device using your device's native messaging capabilities
  • Your Device's Calendar: Only when you choose to create calendar events or share appointments. These events are stored in your device's calendar app

Important: When you send messages or share calendar events with your contacts, you are in full control of what information is shared. We do not automatically share any data without your explicit action.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's security features. When communicating with our AI service, we use HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit.

Your Rights and Control

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app at any time
  • Edit: You can modify your profile information in the Settings screen, and manage your family & friends list at any time
  • Delete: You can clear all your data using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. You can also individually delete contacts from your family & friends list
  • Permission Control: You can revoke calendar or messaging permissions at any time through your device settings

Children's Privacy

AiMama is intended for use by pregnant individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18.

Medical Disclaimer

AiMama is not a medical device and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. The AI Assistant provides general information only and is not a replacement for consultation with healthcare providers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
