AiMama Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 30, 2025

Introduction

AiMama ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our pregnancy tracking mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Personal Information: Name, due date, birth date, weight, and height
  • Health Tracking Data: Kick counts, weight measurements, appointments, contraction timing
  • Chat Messages: Questions and conversations with our AI assistant

How We Store Your Data

Local Storage: All of your personal information and tracking data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not store this data on our servers.

AI Chat: When you use the AI Assistant feature, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to generate responses. These messages include your name and current pregnancy week for context. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to this data: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized pregnancy tracking and information
  • To calculate your current pregnancy week and due date
  • To generate relevant AI assistant responses based on your pregnancy stage
  • To display your health tracking data and statistics

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Only when you use the AI Assistant feature, to generate responses to your questions

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's security features. When communicating with our AI service, we use HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit.

Your Rights and Control

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app at any time
  • Edit: You can modify your profile information in the Settings screen
  • Delete: You can clear all your data using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app

Children's Privacy

AiMama is intended for use by pregnant individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under 18.

Medical Disclaimer

AiMama is not a medical device and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. The AI Assistant provides general information only and is not a replacement for consultation with healthcare providers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
